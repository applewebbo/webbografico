Menu Scolastico was born from a practical need: knowing every day what my son eats at school, without having to search through Excel files or paper notices. The app lets parents check the canteen menu in a few taps, directly from their smartphone.

Schools can upload their weekly menus via a CSV template, simplifying management even for those without technical skills. Each user can register, follow their school and receive notifications about menu changes.

The project is installable as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for quick access from the phone's home screen. The interface is built with Django, TailwindCSS and HTMX for dynamic updates, with AlpineJS for interactive components.
